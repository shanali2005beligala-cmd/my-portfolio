02 / The thinking behind it

Curiosity should be something you can explore.

The interactive sphere is more than decoration. It represents an idea before it settles into a final shape.

I wanted my portfolio to feel less like a document and more like stepping into a small digital world.

You can grab the sphere, rotate it, and change its colour. That interaction reflects how I like to approach learning: look at an idea from different angles, experiment, and gradually make something useful from it.

I also wanted the design to show a little personality without hiding the important details. My projects, experience, skills, and community involvement are all easy to find, but the experience feels like my own.

“I don't just want to show what I've made. I want to show the curiosity that made me start.”

This portfolio will keep evolving as I learn, build new projects, and discover what I want to explore next.
